Liability Waiver & Release

In consideration of being permitted to rent and/or use an electric bicycle ("e-bike") and related equipment from Port City E-Bikes ("the Company"), I, the undersigned rider, acknowledge and agree to the following:

1. Assumption of Risk

I understand that riding an e-bike involves inherent risks including but not limited to: falls, collisions with other riders, pedestrians, vehicles or objects; mechanical failure; road, weather, and trail conditions; and the negligence of other parties. I assume full responsibility for these risks.

2. Release of Liability

I, on behalf of myself, my heirs and assigns, hereby release, waive, and discharge Port City E-Bikes, its owners, employees, and agents from any and all liability, claims, demands, or causes of action arising out of or related to any loss, damage, or injury sustained while using rental equipment.

3. Rider Responsibilities

I agree to wear a helmet at all times, obey all North Carolina traffic laws, ride sober, return the e-bike on time and in the condition received, and not operate the bike while under the influence of alcohol or any impairing substance.

4. Age Requirement

I confirm I am at least 18 years of age, or that a parent/guardian is signing on behalf of a minor rider and accepts full responsibility on the minor's behalf.

5. Medical Authorization

I authorize the Company to seek emergency medical care for me in the event of an injury, and I accept financial responsibility for any such care.
