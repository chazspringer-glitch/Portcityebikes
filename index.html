Safety & Waiver

Your safety is our top priority — every rider, every ride.

  • Helmets always included — fitted before you roll.
  • Safety walk-through — bike controls, brakes, motor assist explained.
  • Route recommendations — safer paths and trails.
  • Riders must be 18+ to operate. Kids welcome with adult supervision.
  • Sober riding only. No riding under the influence.
  • Follow all NC traffic laws — e-bikes follow the same rules as bicycles.

Sign The Waiver

All riders must sign a quick liability waiver before every rental. Takes about 2 minutes — we'll send you the link when you book.
